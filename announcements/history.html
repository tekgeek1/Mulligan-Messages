Release history

What's been added to Mulligan Pro, from the first test builds to today. Newest at the top.

2026

  1. Coming soon

    Review Shots, your own club distances, and a better distance pin

    • Review Shots: after a hole, fix a wrong club, drag shots into the right order, move a shot on the map, or add one the app missed. Putts and penalty strokes are editable too, and you can do it for a round you added by hand.
    • Your own club distances: type in how far you hit a club. It stays separate from the average Mulligan figures out from your shots.
    • Distance pin: it now lands exactly where you tap, and you can drag it.
    Watch the videos

  2. November 2026

    Version 9.21 Released

    • New shield icon across the app and the Apple Watch
    • Delete Account moved into Settings, and your personal golf settings now live on your Profile
    • Mini Driver added, plus 7 Wood and 9 Wood in every club list

  3. October 2026

    Scorecard preview, shot log export, and an easier Add Round

    • View Scorecard: a read-only scorecard you can open without leaving the hole
    • Shot log PDF: export a one-page summary of every shot in a round
    • Add Round is easier to find
    • 6 Hybrid added to setup and to the club distance chart
    • Holes with no known handicap show a dash instead of a made-up number
    • Onboarding fits small iPhones, including the SE

    New states went live this month too, including Michigan, Oklahoma, Nebraska, New Mexico, Indiana and Wisconsin.

  4. September 2026

    Voice assistant, friends on the course, and a Home redesign

    • Voice assistant: press and hold to ask a question or get a club suggestion. It's off until you turn it on.
    • Friend is playing: a badge on Home shows when a friend is out on the course
    • Feature preferences: one screen to switch Health, Friends, Voice, appearance and shot tracking on or off
    • Apple Health status and toggle added to Settings
    • Club changes are now detected from distance only, which cuts down on false club switches
    • On-green detection widened from 15 to 20 yards after a report from a course in Minnesota

  5. August 2026

    Cleaner club data and safer End Round

    • Mishits no longer drag down your club averages
    • Better club suggestions: driver off the tee, sensible wedge distances, no phantom yardages
    • End Round takes a deliberate press, so a pocket tap can't finish your round
    • 2 Iron added to the club lists
    • Real par-3 courses now show up in search and Nearby

  6. July 2026

    Rangefinder mode, Wedge Coach, and 600+ new courses

    • Rangefinder mode: distances only, no scorekeeping
    • Wedge Coach: the Coach's Pick tells you what swing length to use for a wedge
    • Coach's Pick: redesigned club recommendation that waits until you've moved before suggesting
    • One-tap putting when you're on the green
    • Apple Health saves that fail now tell you instead of failing quietly
    • Your Apple Watch ends its session when the round ends

    Read: 600+ new courses · Read: You asked, I listened

  7. June 2026

    Version 9.9: AI Coach, a new Home, and 9-hole rounds Released

    • AI Coach insights: feedback on your game, using Strokes Gained
    • Redesigned Home with Play and Stats dials
    • Coverage map: see which states have courses ready
    • 9-hole rounds
    • Switch modes mid-round without losing your score
    • Rounds survive locking your phone or switching apps
    • GPS keeps running in the background during a round, with a Battery Saver option
    • A short tutorial for first-time users
    • Live distance for the shot you just hit, and a clear mark on mishits
    • Club averages use your typical full swing, so partial shots don't skew them

  8. April – May 2026

    Versions 9.1 and 9.2, ads removed

    • Rounds, scoring and GPS got a big round of fixes
    • Club bag syncs across your devices
    • Featured courses, and search that works better
    • Push notifications and an in-app notification bell
    • Request a course right from the app
    • A round mode picker, so you choose how much the app tracks
    • Ads removed
    • In-round coachmarks that point out the main controls

  9. February – March 2026

    Search and polish

    • Course search shows a full-screen list instead of a map
    • When a search finds nothing, there's a link to request the course
    • General UI cleanup

  10. January 2026

    Course reviews, Dynamic Island, and club suggestions

    • Course reviews with photos, rating breakdowns and a quick or detailed option
    • Live Activities: your score on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island
    • Club suggestions based on how far you hit each club
    • New scorecard design
    • Sign-in stays put when you relaunch the app

2025

  1. December 2025

    Stats dashboard and a new Home

    • A full stats dashboard with filters and a score trend chart
    • Rate course conditions after you play
    • Expandable cards for your recent rounds
    • New hero section on Home

  2. November 2025

    Friends and profiles

    • Profiles with a photo you can take with your camera
    • See a friend's scorecard
    • A personal greeting and quick actions on Home
    • More Apple Watch improvements

  3. October 2025

    The first builds

    • A shared course database, so a course added once is there for everyone
    • GPS distances to the front, middle and back of the green
    • Club bag with average distance per club
    • Apple Watch app with your score and one-tap voice
    • Sign in with Apple, so your clubs follow you if you reinstall
    • Renamed to Mulligan on October 24
